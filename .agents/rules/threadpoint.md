# Threadpoint Rules

- Keep threadpoint provider-neutral, local-first, and suitable for a public
  open-source repository.
- Keep durable shared guidance in `AGENTS.md`, `.agents/knowledge/`, and
  `.agents/rules/`.
- Stage provider-native content under `.agents/imports/agent-artifacts/` before
  moving it into canonical shared files.
- Keep bridge files small and focused on pointing agents at the shared
  threadpoint layout.
